Community Rules

Respectful, focused aur useful NEET student community ke rules.

1. Respect first

Gaali, bullying, hate, personal attack aur harassment allowed nahi hai.

2. Study-focused content

NEET doubts, notes, strategy aur motivation share karein. Repeated spam, promotion aur unrelated flooding na karein.

3. Safe replies

Kisi ka phone number, address, password ya private information post na karein. Fake identity ya impersonation mana hai.

4. Helpful reactions

Like, heart, laugh, fire aur idea reactions responsibly use karein; kisi ko target karne ke liye reaction spam na karein.

5. Content integrity

Misleading answers, malicious links, cheating tools ya unauthorized copyrighted uploads avoid karein. Serious violation admin action ka reason ban sakta hai.
